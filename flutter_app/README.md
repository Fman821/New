# Nur (ኑሩል አዝካር) - Islamic Azkar, Dua & Tasbih Flutter App

A complete, modern Flutter application for Islamic Azkar, Quranic & Prophetic Duas, 99 Names of Allah (Asmaul Husna), and an interactive Tasbih counter with Amharic translations and 5 visual theme modes.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **5 Theme Modes**: Sacred Light (የቀን ብርሃን), Midnight Ottoman (የኦቶማን ሰማያዊ), Glassmorphism (ዘመናዊ ግላስ), Masjid Architecture (የመስጊድ ቅስት), and Dark Emerald & Neon (ኤመራልድ ኒዮን).
- **Azkar Categories**: Morning (24), Noon/After Salah (6), Evening (22), House (13), Travel (6), and Other (12).
- **Interactive Azkar Card**: Audio streaming, repetition tracking with checkmark on completion, favorite bookmarking, and text sharing.
- **Modern Tasbih Counter**: Custom circular painter ring, Dhikr presets, targets (33, 99, 100, 1000), haptic vibration feedback, and lap counter.
- **99 Names of Allah**: Arabic with full tashkeel, English transliteration, and Amharic meaning.
- **Duas & Favorites**: Filterable essential Duas and instant access to bookmarked adhkar.
